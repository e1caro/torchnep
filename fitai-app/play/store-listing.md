## English

**Name:** FitQuest – Train like a hero

**Short description (max 80):** Train like a game: workout plan, dungeons, quests, meals and friends.

**Full description:**

FitQuest turns your training into an adventure. Answer a few questions and get a weekly plan made for you (gym,
dumbbells or just your body weight), fitted to the time you have each day.

• A training plan for your goal, level, equipment and time.
• 950+ exercises with a muscle map, steps and common mistakes.
• Create your own exercises by marking the muscles on the body map and share them in the Guild, the community.
• Level up, complete daily quests and weight goals, and beat the weekly boss.
• Dungeons: special workouts graded S to D, with your friends' ranking.
• Meal plan and shopping list for your calories and tastes.
• A daily reminder at the time you choose.
• 19+ themed interfaces, themes, rings and banners for your profile.
• Works without an account and offline; with an account, sync your data and add friends.

No ads. Estimates are guidance, not medical advice.
